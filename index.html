Where the content comes from

This website does not store or host any comics. Strip images and notes load straight from the Peanuts Wiki on Fandom into your browser each time you read, and nothing is copied to or redistributed by this site. Text from the Peanuts Wiki is community content, generally available under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike license (CC BY-SA) unless a page says otherwise. Each wiki page's history lists its authors.

To load quickly, this website uses an index file that lists which wiki file belongs to each date, along with the wiki's fun-fact notes. The index contains no comic images. The notes are used under CC BY-SA, are credited under each strip, and the index carries the same license.

The logo in the header and the small icon in the browser tab are shown only as personal title graphics and remain the property of their owners.

The site's lettering is "Groundnut", a font © 1999 John K. Barrow (shared as "Aestheticware"), based on the friendly handwriting of Charles "Sparky" Schulz. It is built into this website and its author's terms apply. Its license note asks everyone who uses it to do something to make the world look a little better. More about the designer: John K. Barrow, on Luc Devroye's type design pages. Characters Groundnut does not include, such as numbers and some punctuation, come from "Strip Marker", an original font made for this site and free to use.

Privacy

This website has no accounts, no tracking and no ads, and it does not set cookies itself. Your place, settings and scan results are saved only in your own browser. To show the strips and notes, your browser contacts the Peanuts Wiki (Fandom), and it may load a fallback font from Google Fonts. Those services receive ordinary web requests, such as your IP address, under their own policies.
